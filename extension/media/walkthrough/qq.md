## QQ official bot (optional)

A QQ private chat can control the active Cursor window. Use the official bot and its WebSocket gateway. Unofficial protocols are not supported.

### What you'll do
1. Create a bot on the QQ open platform and choose WebSocket
2. Paste the App ID and App Secret on the **QQ** tab. Turn on **Sandbox** until the bot is approved
3. Open the bot with the console **扫码聊天** code, then send the bind command
4. If the gateway closes with **4914**, Sandbox is still required

The full transcript is not copied into QQ. Approval buttons ride your latest message. Chinese steps: `docs/zh/qq_setup.md`.
